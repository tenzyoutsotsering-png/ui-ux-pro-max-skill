The tradition

A painting is more than an image.

Thangka painting brings together drawing, proportion, iconography, colour, patience and devotion. At Tsering Thangka Studio, these disciplines are taught through close, practical guidance in a small working atelier in McLeod Ganj.

The studio welcomes beginners for short workshops and offers focused longer-term training for students who want to go deeper.

The master

Bhuchung Tsering

With more than four decades of experience, Master Bhuchung Tsering has devoted his practice to the discipline of traditional Thangka painting. His work has been exhibited in India and across Europe.

“The discipline of the line is where the painting begins.”

The studio is intentionally small and personal, allowing students to learn through observation, correction and sustained practice.
