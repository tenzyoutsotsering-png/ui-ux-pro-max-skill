# Cultural Editorial

- Explain the tradition through practice: line, proportion, iconography, pigment, shading, detail, and gold.
- Present teachers and lineage with precise, respectful wording. Do not invent credentials, history, or religious claims.
- Balance educational context with visual storytelling. Use short editorial passages, captions, timelines, process notes, and student journeys instead of walls of text.
- Create a clear information architecture: Home, Courses, Course Detail, Studio, Teachers, Gallery, Workshops, Journal, Commissions, Contact.
- Use captions and contextual metadata to make artwork understandable without overwhelming the viewer.
- Treat Tibetan terminology carefully and preserve original terms where they add meaning, with explanations when needed.
- Make the studio feel active: unfinished works, pigments, tools, teacher corrections, student studies, and works in progress.
