# Forms and Conversion

- Keep the primary CTA obvious: Explore Courses, Enquire, or Apply.
- Ask only for information needed at that stage.
- Group fields logically and use explicit labels.
- Show validation close to the relevant field and preserve entered values after errors.
- Make success and next steps unmistakable.
- Design forms for touch first on mobile.
- Include class details, level, duration, format, location, and what happens after submitting when relevant.
- Never use manipulative urgency, fake scarcity, or confusing opt-ins.
